\# PROJECT\_CONTEXT.md



\## Business Context

We are adapting this app as part of our own product/business roadmap.

The goal is not just to make a technical fork, but to turn it into a usable branded product entry point.



\## Target Users

\- end users of our hardware products

\- non-expert users who need guided flows

\- some advanced internal use for debugging and setup



\## Product Direction

Short term:

\- Android-first MVP

\- complete branding replacement

\- simplified UI

\- support our own hardware defaults



Mid term:

\- onboarding wizard

\- presets for our hardware

\- simplified configuration and diagnostics

\- customer-friendly structure



Long term:

\- product ecosystem app rather than a generic control tool



\## What Matters Most

\- speed to usable MVP

\- maintainability

\- clear user-facing experience

\- ability to ship under our own brand

\- ability to gradually build proprietary value on top of upstream code



\## What Does Not Matter Right Now

\- perfect cross-platform parity

\- supporting every legacy feature

\- broad speculative refactors
